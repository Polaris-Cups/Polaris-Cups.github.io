✦ABOUT

Cups is the creative and technological suit of Polaris — four members, one visual language, and the systems they build.

Polaris is the fixed point: direction, precision, the star you navigate by. Cups is its element of water — intuition, craft and the making of things. Where the two meet, engineering becomes a practice rather than a task.

This archive is not a company page. It is a deck. Each member is a card of the Minor Arcana, each card carries a discipline, and behind every card sits the work that proves it.
